# Interpretation review and revision

Give the reviewer one entry, the project question, source/cache paths and known
extraction limitations. Disclose relevant co-authorship without describing the
desired interpretation. Do not pass sibling verdicts or the drafter's reasoning.

Read the source in context, not just the selected spans. Review every point,
the summary and the positioning. Look for stronger certainty than the source,
generalisation beyond its population/conditions, missing comparison baselines,
incorrect denominators, omitted contrary findings, and project relevance that
depends on an unstated assumption. Check tables visually when extraction is
incomplete. A claim needing another source is not checkable from this one.

Return an anchored disposition for each reviewed component: supported,
overstated, contradicted, or not checkable. Explain the smallest correction and
what evidence would change it. Report the number of components checked and
unread source regions. Do not turn consensus, a label count, or a clean quote
check into a certificate that the entry is valid.

For revision, reopen each disputed source passage. Record applied, adapted or
declined corrections with evidence. Do not mechanically accept all reviewer
suggestions. Preserve unchanged exact spans; any new span runs through the
checker and, for OCR, visual verification again. Summary/positioning edits need
interpretation review even when no quotation changed. An unresolved disagreement
stays visible in the final note.
