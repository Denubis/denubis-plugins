# Review facets

Select facets from the manuscript and brief. They are independent questions with real
dependencies, not a serial schedule or coverage matrix. Record exact evidence for a
concern and state uncertainty where the source cannot settle it. Do not manufacture CLEAR
entries for unaffected units.

## Argument and implications

- Identify the propositions the manuscript asks the reader to accept, including material
  presuppositions and claims carried by headings, tables, contrasts, or examples.
- Test grounds, inferential warrant, backing, qualification, rebuttal, dependencies, and
  downstream implications.
- Distinguish what is stated, entailed, invited, speculative, overextended, or contradicted.
- For a claimed omission or contradiction, name the text that would falsify the concern,
  search the complete in-scope manuscript, and preserve the strongest competing reading.

[Toulmin analysis](toulmin-analysis.md) is an optional structure for a genuinely complex
argument. Do not apply it exhaustively when ordinary claim tracing settles the question.

## Critical appraisal

- Trace question or thesis through design or interpretive approach, sampling or corpus,
  measurement or coding, analysis, results, and conclusion.
- Apply randomisation, comparators, blinding, power, preregistration, reproducibility, and
  statistical criteria only when the design makes them relevant.
- Check selection, attrition, stopping, missingness, assumptions, multiplicity,
  confounding, sensitivity, contrary evidence, and bias at the applicable boundary.
- Separate statistical evidence from substantive importance and internal validity from
  transfer or applicability.
- Check ethics, consent, confidentiality, conflicts, and research-integrity disclosures
  where applicable.

Select design- and discipline-appropriate appraisal guidance. Do not total checklist
scores or let desired outcomes weaken methods scrutiny.

## Transparency and reconstructability

- Can a competent reader reconstruct material collection, preprocessing, coding, analysis,
  exclusions, deviations, and sample or corpus accounting?
- Are data, code, instruments, protocols, and access restrictions described at the level
  the field, law, and ethics permit?
- Are exploratory, planned, preregistered, and post hoc decisions distinguished?
- Are effect or parameter estimates, variation, and uncertainty reported appropriately?
- What expertise or source is unavailable, and which claim does that limit?

Procedural reproducibility is not a universal model for qualitative, interpretive,
theoretical, or humanities scholarship.

## Cited-source fidelity

Use [source-fidelity.md](source-fidelity.md). Split grouped citations into the individual
claim-source relationships the sentence asserts. Judge what each source supports at the
manuscript's strength and scope. Keep support, citation placement, formatting, and page-
locator convention as separate questions.

## Coherence and cold-reader self-sufficiency

- What function does each consequential paragraph serve, and which claim does it advance?
- Does its opening expose that function, do its sentences belong, and does its information
  order match dependencies?
- Are definitions, antecedents, transitions, labels, figures, and cross-references usable?
- Does contrastive, defensive, or corrective prose introduce the alternative it rejects
  and explain why that distinction matters on the page?
- Would deleting the opposition leave the positive claim complete, or remove a real
  qualification or rebuttal?

A contrast marker or long paragraph is a trigger to inspect, not a defect.

## Register and mechanics

Apply the project .notes/ register and current venue. Check voice, stance, terminology,
definitions, tense, person, attribution, grammar, punctuation, headings, captions, lists,
tables, figures, references, and literal material. Distinguish factual or substantive
queries from editorial corrections. Do not add, remove, or restyle citations during a
diagnostic review.

## Concision

Check duplicated claims, redundant examples, throat-clearing, manuscript-management
metadiscourse, stacked qualifications, and residue from deleted argument branches. For a
proposed cut, state what evidence, warrant, orientation, qualification, or voice would be
lost. A grammatical remainder is not proof the cut is safe.

## Reviewer self-audit

State expertise and source gaps, inapplicable criteria, and any effect of outcome,
novelty, prestige, familiarity, or confirmation bias on scrutiny. Do not claim independent
review when a reader saw the desired verdict or sibling findings first.
