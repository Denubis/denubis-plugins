# Evidence base and adaptation limits

This file records why the skill asks its core questions. It is provenance, not
a substitute for discipline-specific reporting standards or reviewer
expertise.

## Parker et al. (2018)

`parkerEmpoweringPeerReviewers2018`

Parker et al., “Empowering peer reviewers with a checklist to improve
transparency,” DOI `10.1038/s41559-018-0545-z`.

Supports the `TRN` lane and reviewer self-audit:

- a checklist facilitates rather than replaces expert judgment
  [@parkerEmpoweringPeerReviewers2018, p. 1];
- criteria may be inapplicable and uncertainty should prompt an author query or
  specialist escalation [@parkerEmpoweringPeerReviewers2018, p. 2];
- exact sample accounting, replicable methods, durable artifacts, complete
  analysis reporting, observation-bias controls, stopping-rule provenance, and
  analysis-plan deviations require scrutiny
  [@parkerEmpoweringPeerReviewers2018, pp. 2–4];
- methods must be judged independently of significance, effect direction, or
  conformity with predictions [@parkerEmpoweringPeerReviewers2018, p. 4];
- effect magnitude, uncertainty, power, and prior evidence should calibrate
  claims without making null, low-powered, or surprising findings automatic
  rejection grounds [@parkerEmpoweringPeerReviewers2018, pp. 4–6].

Limits: a perspective from empirical ecology/evolution, not a validated
universal checklist. Adapt NHST, power, preregistration, and reproducibility
criteria to the manuscript's field. It does not cover qualitative,
interpretive, theoretical, or humanities methods comprehensively.

## Santhanam, Ravindran, and Wincup (2025)

`santhanamCriticalAppraisalOriginal2025`

Santhanam, Ravindran, and Wincup, “Critical appraisal of an original research
article,” DOI `10.1177/14782715251369964`.

Supports `APP` and dependency ordering:

- reporting compliance and methodological appraisal are distinct
  [@santhanamCriticalAppraisalOriginal2025, p. 2];
- question, design, methods, bias/confounding, results, interpretation, ethics,
  and applicability require linked appraisal
  [@santhanamCriticalAppraisalOriginal2025, pp. 3–6];
- serious internal-validity defects are not repaired by good writing or desired
  results [@santhanamCriticalAppraisalOriginal2025, pp. 3, 10];
- appraisal tools must be selected by question and design, used within their
  limits, and supplemented by contextual applicability
  [@santhanamCriticalAppraisalOriginal2025, p. 6];
- aggregate scores can erase differences between substantively unequal defects
  [@santhanamCriticalAppraisalOriginal2025, pp. 6–7].

Limits: a narrative, clinically oriented overview. PICO, NNT, clinical
significance, biomedical ethics examples, and design safeguards are conditional,
not universal. Its statistical guidance is introductory and does not replace a
design-specific statistical review.

## Butcher, Drake, and Leach (2006)

`butcherButchersCopyEditingCambridge2006`

Butcher, Drake, and Leach, *Butcher's Copy-Editing*, fourth edition, print ISBN
`9780521847131`.

Supports the late `REG` lane and its boundary:

- define copy-editing scope and intervention depth before work (§§1.1, 3.2,
  pp. 1–4, 30–32);
- make author queries clear and answerable (§3.3, pp. 33–35);
- verify completeness, version integrity, internal cross-references, and
  self-explanatory copy before detailed correction (§3.4, pp. 36–44);
- maintain internal organisation and consistency through an explicit style
  record (§3.5, pp. 45–49);
- preserve source integrity during on-screen operations and finish with a
  systematic check (§16.7, pp. 427–431; Appendix 1, pp. 432–452).

Limits: a 2006 handbook centred on book production. Do not transfer obsolete
software, typesetting, hard-copy, production-department, or cost advice into a
modern manuscript workflow. Local `.notes` registers and current venue rules
override its house-style examples. Its role here is to structure editorial
checks, not to judge validity or evidence.

## Adaptation limit

The sources motivate review questions; they do not validate a fixed schedule, worker
count, all-paragraph coverage matrix, or universal independence among facets. Choose
methods proportionately to the manuscript and brief. Project scratchpads are application
records, not normative workflow evidence.
