# Paper Review

## Review contract

Review and discuss; do not silently edit the manuscript. A fluent passage, significant
result, checklist total, or model consensus cannot settle validity or source fidelity.
Separate confirmed defects, plausible concerns, competing readings, and unresolved
uncertainty. Ask one pointed question only when authorial intent or authority is genuinely
needed.

## Lock the review surface proportionately

Resolve the canonical manuscript, supplements, tables, figures, response letters, project
instructions, .notes/ register, venue, review mode, depth, and exclusions. Record current
Git state and uncommitted changes. Hash or copy artifacts only when concurrent change or
external transfer makes content identity consequential; do not create a manifest as
ceremony.

Load academic-writing. Load using-bibliography before any Zotero, rendered-paper,
quotation, or citation-fidelity work. Select reporting or appraisal guidance from the
actual study or argument design and current venue. Missing venue guidance blocks only the
venue-specific claim it would settle.

Identify the question or thesis, evidence, methods or interpretive approach, principal
claims, and conclusion before detailed review. Use stable headings plus short opening-text
anchors where line numbers may drift.

## Cover independent review facets

Apply only facets relevant to the brief, but do not let one substitute for another:

- **Argument:** grounds, inferential warrant, qualifications, rebuttals, dependencies, and
  implications actually licensed by the text. Use the optional
  [Toulmin analysis](references/toulmin-analysis.md) when a disputed inference needs its
  parts made explicit.
- **Appraisal:** fit among question, design, sampling or corpus, measurement, analysis,
  internal validity, ethics, interpretation, and applicability.
- **Transparency:** whether a competent reader can reconstruct the material decisions,
  data or corpus accounting, exclusions, analyses, deviations, and access limits.
- **Source fidelity:** whether every reviewed source supports the proposition attributed
  to it at the manuscript's strength and scope.
- **Coherence and self-sufficiency:** paragraph function, information order, definitions,
  transitions, and whether contrasts or defences make sense from the page rather than an
  off-page review history.
- **Register and mechanics:** the attested voice, venue, terminology, cross-references,
  tables, figures, quotations, and literal material.
- **Concision:** repetition and metadiscourse whose removal does not discard evidence,
  warrant, qualification, orientation, or voice.

The detailed facet questions live in [review-lanes.md](references/review-lanes.md).
They are lenses, not a fixed
schedule, score, or requirement to fill every cell with CLEAR.
The [evidence base](references/evidence-base.md) records the methodological sources and
limits behind the procedure; it is rationale, not another compulsory review stage.

## Order by dependency, not phases

Resolve validity and transparency defects before polishing prose they may invalidate.
Establish manuscript claims before auditing what citations support. Run register and
concision work after substantive conclusions are stable. Other facets may be read
independently and synthesized when their evidence overlaps.

The main session may review directly. Delegate a bounded facet only when the user or task
permits it and independent context adds signal. Give every reviewer the same content
boundary, only its facet question, and a required exact-evidence return. Do not require a
worker count or reveal sibling findings before independence matters. The main session
verifies each returned lead.

For source fidelity, use
[source-fidelity.md](references/source-fidelity.md) and the bibliography workflow. An inaccessible
source is UNVERIFIED, not adverse evidence.

## Promote evidence-backed findings

For each candidate, re-open the current text, state the smallest supported defect and
concrete consequence, search for the strongest textual recovery or competing reading, and
classify the remaining uncertainty. Merge findings that require the same author decision;
batch local unambiguous faults. Do not promote optional polish, speculative implications,
or a trigger with no consequence.

Each author-facing finding contains:

- exact manuscript anchor and relevant source;
- minimum diagnosis and concrete consequence;
- strongest competing reading;
- evidence or observation that would change the assessment; and
- required response: evidence, author decision, editorial action, or acknowledged reviewer
  limit.

There is no finding quota, rejection rate, fixed review loop, or aggregate score.
[Promotion triage](references/promotion-triage.md) provides a falsification rubric, not a
status certificate.

## Completion

Recheck that every in-scope central claim and named review question received the relevant
facet coverage; do not demand an all-paragraph-by-all-facet matrix. Preserve disagreements
that evidence does not resolve. Verify source identities and quotations through the
bibliography procedure, and state inaccessible material or expertise limits.

Return findings ordered by consequence, followed by strengths relevant to interpretation,
questions requiring the author, sources and checks used, and the bounded surface not
inspected. Do not edit until the author selects a finding or requests a revision pass.
