# AUTHOR — prose register (PROJECT)

**Provenance.** SLOT: where these rules come from, with enough
specificity that a later session can re-verify them. Name the corpus
or exemplar papers (how many, which years, roughly how many words),
who derived the rules and when, and where the fuller derivation
lives. A register derived from one target paper is a legitimate
start, but say so, because single-exemplar derivation cannot
distinguish stable voice from venue imposition or from a register
the author has since abandoned.

**Attestation basis.** SLOT: for each rule below that makes a
countable claim (a rate, a ceiling, a mean), note what it rests on —
"measured across N papers", "observed in the exemplar", or "author
preference, stated DATE". Unattested rules are permitted; unlabelled
ones are not, because the skill cannot tell a measured target from a
guess.

**Recency.** SLOT: date of the most recent evidence, and any known
drift — a mark or pattern the author used historically but has
deliberately dropped (or adopted) since. State which era wins for
new prose.

## Voice and stance

SLOT: person and its variation; reader address; hedging calibration
(what carries epistemic weight and stays); intensifiers and
boosters; concession frequency; attribution-verb conventions.

## Sentence and paragraph construction

SLOT: sentence-length target; subordinator and connective habits;
signposting conventions the venue or author attests (sequential
markers, section openers); paragraph-length register; tense
conventions for prior work, methods, and claims.

## Punctuation

SLOT: the project's actual hierarchy — em-dash, semicolon, colon,

only a frequency. "Permitted, not promoted" is a legitimate setting.

## Lexis and orthography

SLOT: spelling standard, serial-comma rule, acronym handling,

## Citations

SLOT: the venue's citation style and the division of labour — in

## Known generation biases

SLOT (optional but valuable): directions the drafting model is known

over-length sentences, or performing voice with figurative flourish.
