## Cluster A — Critique, peer review, adversarial evaluation, LLM-as-judge (DOIs verified)

### Scholarly peer review: reliability & bias
- Mahoney, M. J. (1977). Publication prejudices: An experimental study of confirmatory bias in the peer review system. *Cognitive Therapy and Research*, 1(2), 161–175. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01173636 — institutional
- Bornmann, L., Mutz, R., & Daniel, H.-D. (2010). A reliability-generalization study of journal peer reviews: A multilevel meta-analysis of inter-rater reliability and its determinants. *PLoS ONE*, 5(12), e14331. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0014331 — open access
- Jefferson, T., Alderson, P., Wager, E., & Davidoff, F. (2002). Effects of editorial peer review: A systematic review. *JAMA*, 287(21), 2784–2786. https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.287.21.2784 — institutional (Cochrane alternative: https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.MR000016.pub3)

### Modern code review
- Bacchelli, A., & Bird, C. (2013). Expectations, outcomes, and challenges of modern code review. *Proc. ICSE 2013*, 712–721. https://doi.org/10.1109/ICSE.2013.6606617 — institutional
- Rigby, P. C., & Bird, C. (2013). Convergent contemporary software peer review practices. *Proc. ESEC/FSE 2013*, 202–212. https://doi.org/10.1145/2491411.2491444 — institutional

### Structured analytic technique / red teaming
- Heuer, R. J., Jr. (1999). *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis.* CIA Center for the Study of Intelligence. No DOI (gov pub, public domain). Ch. 8 = Analysis of Competing Hypotheses.

### Calibration & forecasting
- Tetlock, P. E. (2005/2009). *Expert Political Judgment: How Good Is It? How Can We Know?* Princeton UP. https://doi.org/10.1515/9781400830312 — DOI is the 2009 e-edition; work usually cited 2005. **Flag the year.**
- Mellers, B., et al. (2014). Psychological strategies for winning a geopolitical forecasting tournament. *Psychological Science*, 25(5), 1106–1115. https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614524255 — institutional

### Argumentation theory
- Mercier, H., & Sperber, D. (2011). Why do humans reason? Arguments for an argumentative theory. *Behavioral and Brain Sciences*, 34(2), 57–74. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X10000968 — institutional
- Toulmin, S. E. (2003). *The Uses of Argument* (Updated ed.). Cambridge UP. https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511840005 — DOI is 2003 ed.; original 1958 has no DOI. **Flag the edition.**

### LLM-as-judge & multi-agent debate (CS preprints / conference)
- Zheng, L., et al. (2023). Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena. *NeurIPS 2023 D&B*. arXiv:2306.05685 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685
- Du, Y., et al. (2023). Improving Factuality and Reasoning in Language Models through Multiagent Debate. arXiv:2305.14325 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14325
- Liang, T., et al. (2023). Encouraging Divergent Thinking in LLMs through Multi-Agent Debate. *EMNLP 2024*. arXiv:2305.19118 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19118
- Wang, P., et al. (2023). Large Language Models are not Fair Evaluators. arXiv:2305.17926 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17926 (judge position-bias / failure mode)
